## Research & Technical Experience

### Brookhaven National Laboratory — Neutrino Physics

Conducted research associated with the Short-Baseline Neutrino Program, applying C++/ROOT and scientific analysis methods to neutrino energy reconstruction.

- Investigated charged-current quasielastic (CCQE) energy estimation.
- Produced reconstructed-energy comparisons and scientific visualizations.
- Contributed to research documentation and poster presentation.

### Computational Biology & Bioinformatics

Developing analytical workflows for biological sequence processing and structured storage of amino-acid and sequence information.

- FASTA sequence parsing and validation.
- Python, R, and SQL-based data processing.
- Research into mutation effects and protein structure.

### Computational Physics & Numerical Modeling

Research experience in numerical simulation and mathematical modeling across particle physics, compact astrophysical objects, and theoretical physics.

- Pulsar and boson-star binary simulations.
- Shapiro time-delay analysis.
- Numerical computation and scientific visualization.

## Development Philosophy

> Clean workflows. Reproducible research. Meaningful results.

- **Reproducibility:** Document data sources, preprocessing, experimental settings, and execution steps.
- **Model evaluation:** Compare methods using appropriate metrics, baselines, and validation techniques.
- **Data visualization:** Communicate results using clear, interpretable figures and dashboards.
- **Scientific rigor:** Identify assumptions, limitations, and sources of uncertainty.
- **Software quality:** Emphasize maintainable code, verification, and documentation.
